## What I understood
- Vector search is a technique to store, group, search, and compare items based on their vector representations (vector embeddings).
- It is also called "similarity searc" or "nearest neighbor search" because it facilitates grouping and matching of items.
- Its implementation on database systems is based on the Vector Space Model.
